# 0002: Estate Assistant Open Questions

**Type:** Note
**Date:** 2026-10-18

These questions block replacing the HR content listed in product decision [0001](./0001-estate-assistant-direction.md). Record each answer as a product decision and link it here.

1. **Users.** Who asks the assistant: residents (warga/penghuni), management staff (pengelola, security, admin), or both? This decides the role, the tone, and what the assistant may disclose to whom.
2. **Complex type.** Landed housing or cluster, apartment (rumah susun), or mixed with shophouses? This decides which regulations replace the labor-law context. Candidates to verify: UU 1/2011 on housing and its implementing rules for landed housing; UU 20/2011 and Permen PUPR 14/2021 (PPPSRS) for rumah susun.
3. **Data source.** Fictional mock house rules, labelled `MOCK DATA` like the HR version, or the real house rules (tata tertib), IPL amounts, and management contacts?
4. **Topics.** One topic per eval suite, replacing working time, leave, payroll, and benefits. Proposed topics:
   - IPL and payments.
   - Security, guests, and parking.
   - Facilities and cleanliness.
   - Renovation and complaints.
